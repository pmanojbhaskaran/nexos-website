How to request deletion

User accounts are created or provisioned by authorized company, tenant, or workspace administrators. To request deletion of personal or app-related data associated with your NEXOS account, please email support@nexos.co.in with the details below:

  • registered email address used for NEXOS
  • company, tenant, or workspace name
  • your role or relationship with the workspace, if known
  • a brief description of the data deletion request

Verification

For security and tenant-protection reasons, NEXOS / Adyaya Solutions Private Limited will verify the request before processing it. Where your account is managed by an employer, customer, tenant, or workspace administrator, we may need to validate the request with the relevant organization before taking action.

Deletion requests are processed subject to applicable law, contractual obligations, tenant or company administrator approval where relevant, and legitimate business record-retention requirements.

Data that may be retained

Some data may be retained where required for operational continuity, shipment history, invoices, audit logs, security records, dispute resolution, legal, tax, regulatory, or contractual obligations. Not all data associated with an account can always be deleted. Retained data will be handled in accordance with the NEXOS Privacy Policy and applicable law.
