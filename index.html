The Problem

Your operations deserve better than this

Most Indian courier and co-loader operators run daily operations through Excel, WhatsApp, Tally, and local software. This creates movement without accountability, billing without margin truth, and handover without proof.

Excel Chaos

Booking registers, tracking sheets, billing files — scattered across laptops. One wrong row and an entire consignment is lost.

WhatsApp Operations

Pickup confirmations, POD photos, exception alerts — buried in chat groups with no audit trail and no accountability.

Tally-Only Billing

Billing disconnected from operations. No real-time margin visibility. Leakage discovered weeks later — or never.

Local Software Limits

Single-machine software with no mobile support, no multi-branch control, no co-loader workflow, and no upgrade path.

Why NEXOS

Built by operators. Not by coders.

NEXOS is shaped by structured workflow design for courier and co-loader operations — not inflated traction claims on a marketing site. Every workflow reflects real booking, pickup, hub, delivery, billing, and margin-control problems.

How most operators work today

  • Booking in Excel, tracking on WhatsApp, billing in Tally — nothing connected
  • No live shipment visibility once dispatched
  • POD photos buried in WhatsApp groups — no audit trail
  • Co-loader handover responsibility unclear until dispute
  • Billing leakage discovered weeks later, if at all
  • Attendance only for riders — ops, hub, finance, HR ignored
  • Local software locked to one machine — no mobile, no multi-branch

How NEXOS changes this

  • One chain from booking to billing — every step connected and auditable
  • Live status at every scan point — hub, bag, manifest, delivery
  • ePOD with mandatory proof fields — OTP, signature, photo, timestamp
  • Co-loader responsibility derived from booking configuration automatically
  • Real-time billing, payable, and margin visibility per shipment
  • All-department attendance — ops, hub, airport, HR, finance, admin, riders
  • Web + mobile + multi-branch from day one — no hardware lock-in

Product philosophy

AI-assisted, not AI-dependent. Domain-intelligent by design.

NEXOS embeds logistics operating knowledge into workflows, validation gates, responsibility mapping, and audit trails. AI may assist with summaries, guidance, and exception review, but core operations remain rule-driven, deterministic, and reliable.

Core booking, movement, POD, COD, billing, and responsibility flows must continue to operate without AI.

No API or AI dependency for core operations. NEXOS is BOS + rules + workflow first. AI is assistive, optional, and never required for daily operations.
